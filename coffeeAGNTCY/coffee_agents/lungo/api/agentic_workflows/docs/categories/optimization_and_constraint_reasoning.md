# Optimization & Constraint Reasoning

Explicit trade-off reasoning under multi-objective budgets. Patterns in this category optimize under cost, time, carbon,
or similar currencies—surfacing Pareto fronts instead of a single fake optimum when objectives genuinely conflict.

**Patterns:** Economic Constraint Agent

Distinct from **Governance, Policy & Human Oversight** (hard rules and approvals) and **Orchestration & Control Flow**
(saga structure)—this category names *which trade-offs are acceptable* in comparable units before a plan commits.
