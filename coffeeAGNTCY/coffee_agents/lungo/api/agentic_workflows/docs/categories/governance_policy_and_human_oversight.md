# Governance, Policy & Human Oversight

Guardrails, challenger voices, and human accountability before irreversible action. Patterns in this category block or
condition automation against published rules, stress-test proposals before commitment, or pause for attributable,
auditable human approval when stakes exceed autonomy.

**Patterns:** Policy-Enforced Execution, Approval Gate Agent, Adversarial Review Agents

**Not to be confused with Orchestration & Control Flow** - governance patterns constrain *whether* an action may proceed;
orchestration patterns structure *how* work moves once it is allowed.
